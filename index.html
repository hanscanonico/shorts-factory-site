Shorts Factory

A personal, self-hosted tool that produces short vertical videos and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

Shorts Factory is a private command-line pipeline written by Hans Canonico. It writes a short script, records a synthetic voice-over, picks stock footage, adds captions, renders a video, and uploads the result to the YouTube channel of the person running it. It is not a public service and has no users other than its author.

The application asks Google for one permission, youtube.upload, which it uses only to upload videos to the connected channel.
