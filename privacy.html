Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Shorts Factory is a personal, self-hosted application operated by Hans Canonico. It is used only by its author to upload videos to the author's own YouTube channel. This page explains what data it touches.

What Google data the app accesses

The app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope. It uses that permission for exactly one purpose: uploading video files, titles, descriptions and tags to the YouTube channel that authorised it, and scheduling their publication. It does not read channel analytics, comments, subscriber data, or any other Google account information.

What is stored

The OAuth token issued by Google is stored on the author's own computers, protected by file permissions, and used solely to make the uploads described above. The videos and metadata the app produces are stored on the same computers. No data is sent to any third party other than Google (for the upload itself), Pexels (to fetch stock footage), and Microsoft (to synthesise the voice-over from the script text).

What is not collected

The app has no users other than its author, no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, and no advertising. It does not collect, sell, or share personal information about anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access page. Revoking access stops all uploads immediately.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Shorts Factory's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
